Rooted in Heritage

The Cultural Journey of Abhiram

Art for Abhiram D Ajeesh is not merely a practice of form and color—it is an invocation of identity, myth, and emotional resonance. Deeply inspired by classical folklore, murals, tribal symbolism, and the intricate spirit of traditional arts, Abhiram weaves ancestral storytelling into contemporary visual art.

Specializing in both detailed pencil/charcoal sketches and vibrant acrylic/oil paintings, his work reflects a meticulous mastery over light, texture, and rhythm. Each stroke celebrates the vibrant hues of earth ochre, vermilion, peacock blue, and gold leaf.
